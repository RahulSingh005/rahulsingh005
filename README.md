### ReadWatch

</div>

Turns articles and YouTube videos into 150-word Gemini summaries, with a Chrome extension that queues pages as you browse.

- Three services: React frontend, Express + MongoDB API, Flask summarizer
- Manifest V3 extension with a 4-tier Playwright and ScraperAPI fallback that fetched 95% of tested pages
- JWT auth with OTP email verification, follows and likes across 17 REST endpoints

### BlinkChat

</div>

Real-time messenger on MERN and Socket.io, with voice and video calls and AI features built in.

- WebRTC voice and video calls with screen share, 24-hour stories and web push
- JWT and Google OAuth sign-in, Zod validation, rate limiting and 52 backend tests

### Stock Sentiment Analysis

</div>

Tests whether daily news sentiment helps predict GOOGL and MSFT stock prices.

- Scored 46K news headlines from 2023 with VADER and joined them to 250 trading days
- Compared Random Forest, SVM, a Keras MLP and tree regressors
- Decision Tree regressor reached R² 0.92 and $1.04 MAE on same-day GOOGL close
